# still online v0.5.0

**Week two: days 6–10, up to the Velvet Lantern rec hall show.** The story no longer stops after the concert. Five more days of chats, mail, sites and profiles lead to show night, for a girl or a boy, in English and German.

## Highlights

- **Five new days.** Days 6–10 are fully scripted. Every friend has a new chat each day, and the arcs meet on day 10, when Velvet Lantern plays the rec hall and you're "on the list".
- **Show night.** Marco's show album on Pixlog, the Velvet Lantern band page ("played. 83 people"), Mom's mail about meeting Dex and Westfield High's election result all come out on day 10.
- **A poster for your desktop.** Theo's Velvet Lantern show poster becomes a wallpaper after his day-10 chat. Find it in Display Properties.
- **Still a girl's or a boy's story.** A boy gets his own jokes, mail and threads (Marco's load-in crew report), a girl gets hers (including Jess's late-night call and a small step with Theo), and the friends and facts stay the same.

## What's new

- **Marco**: the rec hall date slips ("bingo always wins"), the setlist argument, "we're playing ur song" and the sound check with the upside-down mic.
- **Kayla**: the class rep election against Brianna, the 61–61 tie and the co-reps.
- **Theo**: the poster, from the art wall to the show night.

- **Mail**: 5–6 new mails a day on days 6–10 from Mom, Mr Dalton, Dr. Hargrove, the fan club newsletter and your friends.
- **Sites**: new posts on Westfield High, Tinfoil Comets, Kickflip Kings, Celeb Gossip 07 and CheatCave; Searchly finds them all.
- **Friendspace and Pixlog**: new bulletins every day, wall comments, the Velvet Lantern band page, and one new album per friend, with the show album on day 10.

- **Gender**: three new boy-only and three new girl-only threads, with matching mail and photo captions.
- **German**: all of the above is written for a German 2007, not translated. The German chunk still loads only when you play in German.
- **Your v0.4 saves keep working**: a save on day 5 continues with day 6 after the next shutdown.
- **Documentation**: new [story-week2.md](story-week2.md) and a v0.5 section in [architecture-v0.4.md](architecture-v0.4.md); README updated.

## Known issues

- Stories now cover days 1–10; later days keep the day-10 state.
- The social sites are for reading and decorating, not for posting to strangers.
- Files you created (saved attachments, TuneMole downloads, snapshots) keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.
