# still online v0.4.1

A fix release for v0.4: your profile, a few words, a few pixels and one chat that ran late.

## Changes

- **Your Friendspace profile follows you.** Save Changes now keeps only what you typed. If you only pasted a layout, the headline, mood and "About me" are the defaults again and follow a switch to German or to Boy. Profiles saved with v0.4 are cleaned up on load, and your own text stays.
- **Your picture is your picture.** Friendspace and Pixlog show the picture you chose in User Accounts (on `/me` and in your friends' Top 8), and they update as soon as you change it.
- **Back to the top after Save** on Friendspace `/me`, instead of halfway down your wall.
- **The boy's side got its own things:** the last four diary entries in the Recycle Bin are his, his favorites start with Searchly, CheatCave and Kickflip Kings, and his Friendspace mood is „bored" (N). Untouched favorites from v0.4 still follow the side.
- **Photos have the right date.** The stamp on story photos matches the night they were taken: the Tinfoil Comets concert reads `'07 10 11`.
- **Small fixes:** Jess counts the concert down in days, English text uses American spelling (`homework_math.txt`), the day-2 Pixlog mail no longer counts visitors, the German song notebook rhymes again, „1 obviously" keeps its space on reply buttons, long survey bulletins stay in one column, and a language switch keeps the chat at its last line.
- **Stapley steps aside.** His tip balloon folds away after a few seconds when a window covers his corner, for example a maximized Web Explorer. Click him to read it again; Hide Stapley works as before.
- **No late follow-ups.** A friend's follow-up you never answered no longer arrives after the next day's chat (Dex won't tell you he landed the kickflip 0 times after he landed it).

## Known issues

- Stories still cover days 1–5; later days keep the day-5 state. Days 6–10 come in v0.5.
- The Tinfoil Comets site still says „THIS FRIDAY" (the band's own date), and the day-2 Friendspace mail still counts 3 profile views.
- Files you created (saved attachments, TuneMole downloads, snapshots) keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.

## Auf Deutsch

Ein Fehlerbehebungs-Update für v0.4: Ihr Friendspace-Profil speichert nur noch, was Sie selbst geschrieben haben, und folgt so einem Wechsel der Sprache oder von Mädchen zu Junge; Friendspace und Pixlog zeigen Ihr Bild aus den Benutzerkonten. Der Junge hat jetzt eigene Tagebucheinträge, eigene Favoriten und eine eigene Stimmung („gelangweilt“). Dazu: Fotos tragen das Datum der Story (das Konzert: `'07 10 11`), lange Umfrage-Bulletins bleiben einspaltig, nach einem Sprachwechsel steht der Chat wieder bei der letzten Zeile, Stapleys Sprechblase weicht maximierten Fenstern aus, und unbeantwortete Nachrichten von gestern kommen nicht mehr nach dem Chat von heute.
