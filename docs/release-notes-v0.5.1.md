# still online v0.5.1

A small fix release for v0.5: show dates that keep up with the week, a tidier band page, one line from Pia, pop-ups that switch language, and Stapley out of the way.

## Changes

- **Lakeview is THIS SATURDAY.** From day 6 the Tinfoil Comets tour page lists Lakeview as „THIS SATURDAY!!!" instead of „next saturday", and The Rocket Room moves up to „next week".
- **Past shows are past.** Friendspace band pages split their shows: shows that happened or were replaced leave "Upcoming Shows", and a new "Past Shows" box keeps the recaps, newest first (Riverside, the bean cellar, the rec hall, Lakeview). The Sugarcube Parade booked the fall fair.
- **Pia, day 10, as a boy:** after her „LIAR. i saw the PICS" you answer „ok. busted" instead of „deal".
- **Messenger pop-ups follow a language switch.** A „… says:" or „… has just signed in." pop-up that is up while you switch the language (or Girl/Boy) shows the new text at once.
- **Stapley stands above the status bar** of a maximized window instead of covering its right end.
- **Readable on dark layouts:** the "Autoplay profile songs" box on your Friendspace `/me` now sits on the profile panel, so it stays readable on a starry or other dark pasted layout.

## Known issues

- Stories cover days 1–10; later days keep the day-10 state.
- The social sites are for reading and decorating, not for posting to strangers.
- Files you created (saved attachments, TuneMole downloads, snapshots) keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.
