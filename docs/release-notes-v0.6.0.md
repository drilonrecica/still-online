# still online v0.6.0

**Play on your phone or tablet.** The same Windows XP desktop now works by touch, in portrait and landscape, in English and German. The story is unchanged (days 1–10).

## Highlights

- **Phone:** every window is full screen, the taskbar has icon-only buttons, Start → All Programs is a full-height panel, and the on-screen keyboard never hides what you type into.
- **Tablet:** floating windows you drag by the title bar and resize from the edges, a 40 px taskbar with labels, and flyouts that open by tap.
- **Tap and long-press:** tap opens, long-press is the right-click, and a double-tap on a title bar maximizes.
- **Desktop unchanged:** a mouse and keyboard desktop behaves as in v0.5.

## What's new

- **Web Explorer:** _Fit to screen_ (the phone default) shrinks 2007's fixed-width pages to your screen; _Actual size_ scrolls sideways. A 36 px toolbar and a Favorites sheet.
- **Messenger and Mail:** full-screen chats with the reply row above the keyboard; Mail shows one pane at a time with a Back button.
- **Paint:** draw with one finger, pan with two, long-press a colour for the second colour. **Snapshot Booth** and **Amplifier** are laid out for the phone.
- **Games:** Mines flags by long-press or with the 🚩 button and chords by tapping a number; Star Pinball has a flipper under each thumb; Hen Hunt shoots by tap and pans by drag; Desktop Wrecker gets Reset and Use buttons.
- **Easter eggs:** the Konami code works as swipes, and Fluff can be dragged and petted.
- **Small screens:** the "your screen is small" notice is gone. Old saves keep working.
- **Documentation:** new [touch.md](touch.md) and [architecture-v0.6.md](architecture-v0.6.md); README has a "Play on your phone or tablet" section.
- **Tests:** four Playwright projects (desktop, phone portrait, phone landscape, tablet) run in the default suite.

## Known issues

- Stories cover days 1–10; later days keep the day-10 state.
- The social sites are for reading and decorating, not for posting to strangers.
- Files you created keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor.
- Links on 800 px website pages are small at Fit to screen on a phone (zoom about 0.5); use Actual size or zoom with the toolbar.
- Mines' cells are under 36 px where the board can't fit them (Expert on a phone, Beginner in landscape); flag mode and long-press keep it playable.
- Hen Hunt and Pinball are letterboxed in portrait; landscape is the better way to play Hen Hunt.
- Some desktop looks are left as they are: Notepad's wide menu items, Mail's column header, and the tablet Amplifier's 20 px title bar.

## Auf Deutsch

still online 0.6.0 läuft jetzt auf **Handy und Tablet**. Es ist derselbe XP-Desktop, für Touch angepasst: auf dem Handy füllt jedes Fenster den Bildschirm, ein Tippen öffnet, langes Drücken ersetzt den Rechtsklick, und die Bildschirmtastatur verdeckt nichts. Auf dem Tablet bleiben die Fenster frei verschiebbar und in der Größe änderbar. Der Web Explorer passt die festen 800-Pixel-Seiten mit „An Bildschirm anpassen“ auf den Bildschirm an. Paint malt mit einem Finger und scrollt mit zwei, Minesweeper setzt Flaggen per langem Drücken oder mit dem 🚩-Knopf, und Pinball, Hen Hunt und Amplifier funktionieren per Touch. Alles gibt es im Hoch- und Querformat, auf Deutsch und Englisch. Am Desktop mit Maus ändert sich nichts. Bestehende Spielstände funktionieren weiter. Bekannte Einschränkungen: Links auf den Webseiten sind bei „An Bildschirm anpassen“ klein, und die Minesweeper-Felder sind auf dem Handy bei Experte kleiner als 36 Pixel.
