# still online v0.3.0

**Jetzt auch auf Deutsch.** still online is now fully playable in German as well as English: the desktop, every app and all of the content, written for a German 2007 rather than translated word for word. Switch any time, live.

## Highlights

- **Two languages, one world.** English and German share the same friends, days and storylines. A German browser starts in German; everyone else gets English.
- **Switch without losing anything.** Pick the language on the login screen, with the tray badge, or in the new **Regional and Language Options** app. Open chats, mail, files and windows change language on the spot, with no reload.
- **Cultural localization, not just words.** Friends talk in 2007 German chat-speak, the school becomes a Gymnasium, euros replace dollars, and US-only references are swapped for German ones.
- **Your v0.2 saves keep working.** Chats, files, mail, scores and settings migrate to a language-neutral format on load.

## What's new

- **Language picker** on the login screen (**Language / Sprache**), a **DE/EN** badge in the tray and the new **Regional and Language Options** app (Start menu) with its own icon.
- **Localized shell and apps**: boot, login, Start menu, taskbar, windows, Explorer, Notepad, Paint, Snapshot Booth, Display Properties, Mail, Web Explorer, Amplifier and all four games (in German: Minen, Hühnerjagd, Sternen-Flipper, Desktop-Randale), plus Fluff (Flocke) and Stapley (Tacki).
- **German content** for all six friends (every script, away reply, presence message and game or music reaction), mail for days 1–5, seeded files, photo captions, Spaceport profiles, Searchly, and all the sites (Westfield-Gymnasium, Celeb Gossip 07, the Tinfoil Comets fan page, VirusGuard, and the rest).
- **Locale-aware formatting** for dates, times, numbers and "today / yesterday" in both languages (for example `16:37`, `1.234`).
- **Language-neutral saves**: transcripts, file names, mail and favorites are stored as references and rendered in the current language. Anything you typed or named yourself keeps its language.
- **Layout pass** for German's longer words: dialogs, menus, toolbars, the contact list and Mail's message list.
- **Documentation**: new [docs/i18n.md](i18n.md) and [docs/localization-de.md](localization-de.md); content format and architecture docs updated.

## Known issues

- Stories still cover days 1–5; later days keep the day-5 state.
- A `/tag?t=…` URL opened in English shows no posts on Celeb Gossip after switching to German; tags differ per language.
- Files you created (saved attachments, TuneMole downloads, snapshots) keep the language they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.
