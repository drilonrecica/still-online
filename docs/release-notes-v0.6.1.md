# still online v0.6.1

Three requests after v0.6: a proper picture viewer, desktop icons you can move, and a Desktop Wrecker that hits much harder.

## Changes

- **Picture Viewer.** Opening a picture no longer replaces the folder's contents. It opens in its own **Picture Viewer** window, with Previous / Next through the folder's pictures, Best fit / Actual size, **Edit in Paint** and **Open folder**. There is one viewer: opening another picture shows it there. On a phone or tablet, swipe sideways to go to the next or previous picture. Picture attachments in Mail and the photos in Snapshot Booth's strip open in it too.
- **Move your desktop icons.** Drag an icon (with the mouse, or with a finger on a phone or tablet) and it snaps to the nearest free spot on the desktop grid, as XP's "Align to Grid" does. The layout is saved. Right-click the desktop → **Arrange Icons By ▸ Name** puts everything back in order. A tap, click or double-click still opens and selects as before.
- **Desktop Wrecker, rebuilt.** The same five tools, each with a detailed model and much bigger effects:
  - **Machine Gun:** a real gun, held below-right of the crosshair and recoiling with every shot, with a muzzle flash, tracers to each hit, sparks, dust, flying chips, shell casings, chipped bullet holes and a small screen jolt every few shots.
  - **Chainsaw:** a running chain, a shaking engine and exhaust smoke; cuts are deep, torn gashes, and sparks and sawdust spray out behind the blade.
  - **Flamethrower:** twin tanks and a wand whose flame stream shoots from the nozzle to the target, with embers, smoke and scorch that glows and cools.
  - **Hammer:** a claw hammer that swings down; each blow flashes, sends out a shockwave, blasts glass and chips, and leaves a crushed crater.
  - **Stamp:** a lacquered rubber stamp that squashes down, with a dust ring and ink spatter.
  - New synthesized sounds for all five. With "reduce motion" switched on in your system, there is no shaking, half the particles and dimmer flashes.

## Known issues

- Stories cover days 1–10; later days keep the day-10 state.
- The social sites are for reading and decorating, not for posting to strangers.
- Files you created keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor.

## Auf Deutsch

Drei Wünsche nach v0.6: Bilder öffnen sich jetzt in der eigenen **Bildanzeige** statt im Ordner – mit Zurück/Weiter, Einpassen/Originalgröße, „In Paint bearbeiten“ und „Ordner öffnen“; auf dem Handy wischen Sie zum nächsten Bild. **Desktopsymbole lassen sich verschieben**: Ziehen Sie ein Symbol, und es rastet im Raster ein; die Anordnung wird gespeichert, und „Symbole anordnen nach ▸ Name“ stellt die Ordnung wieder her. **Desktop-Randale** hat neue, detaillierte Werkzeuge und viel stärkere Effekte: Mündungsfeuer, Leuchtspur, Funken, Rauch, Glutfunken, Glassplitter, Schockwellen, tiefe Sägeschnitte und neue Klänge.
