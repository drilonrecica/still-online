# still online v0.4.0

**Friendspace, Pixlog and GlitterCodez, for a girl or a boy.** The social internet of 2007 gets a lot bigger: three new sites to read and decorate, a Top 8 that has feelings, and a choice at the login screen of who you play as. Everything is in English and German.

## Highlights

- **Play as a girl or a boy.** Pick at login or in the new **User Accounts** app, along with your name and one of eight pictures. The six friends, their voices and the story stay the same; what is addressed to or about you changes (files, favorites, chats, mail and a few sites). Switch any time, live, with nothing lost.
- **A Top 8 that reacts.** Drop a friend from your Friendspace Top 8 and they notice: the next day, they bring it up in Messenger and leave a line on your wall. Add them back and they're happy again.
- **Three new social sites.** Friendspace grows bulletins and band pages, Pixlog is a photo-sharing site with a life of its own, and GlitterCodez hands out layouts you can paste into your own profile.
- **Both sides of the 2007 web.** CheatCave and Kickflip Kings join the sites already there, and everything can be found through Searchly.

## What's new

- **User Accounts** (Start menu): your name, picture and Girl / Boy. Files, favorites, Messenger chats and mail that you never changed follow your choice; things you wrote or renamed stay as they are.
- **Gendered content**: compliments, crush talk and topics aimed at you are written for a girl and for a boy, in both languages. A boy gets boyish 2007, a girl gets girly 2007.
- **CheatCave** (`cheatcave.example`): cheats (including for the game's own Mines, Hen Hunt and Star Pinball), flash-game news, funny clips and a forum.
- **Kickflip Kings** (`kickflipkings.example`): a skate crew site with trick tips, crew pages, a shoutbox and Velvet Lantern gig listings. Dex's video premiere is there on day 5.
- **Friendspace** (renamed from Spaceport): a Top 8 editor that takes friends, bands and made-up names, a mood history, **bulletins** (chain letters and news), four **band pages** with a player that sends tracks to the Amplifier, a profile song that autoplays (you can turn it off), and a **layout-code** box for your own page. A dot shows when new bulletins are waiting.
- **Pixlog** (`pixlog.example`): shouts feed, and for each friend a profile, albums with photo comments, a blog, surveys and a guestbook. Give **kudos** (once per friend per day), and on your own page see your visitors, build an album from your pictures, fill in surveys and pick one of six skins.
- **GlitterCodez** (`glittercodez.example`): twelve layouts (girly and boyish, yours first) with previews and a **Copy code** button, plus glitter text, stickers and cursors. Paste a code into your Friendspace profile and the page changes.
- **Safe by design**: layout codes are made-up markup, never CSS or HTML. Only the fixed backgrounds, fonts, stickers and cursors are understood, and anything else is ignored.
- **Your v0.3 saves keep working**: your Top 8, favorites and settings load unchanged.
- **German**: the new sites, bulletins, bands, Pixlog profiles, surveys, layouts and Top 8 reactions are written for a German 2007, not translated.
- **Documentation**: new [docs/gender.md](gender.md) and [docs/architecture-v0.4.md](architecture-v0.4.md); README and credits updated.

## Known issues

- Stories still cover days 1–5; later days keep the day-5 state.
- The social sites are for reading and decorating, not for posting to strangers; you can't add your own band or message people on them.
- Files you created (saved attachments, TuneMole downloads, snapshots) keep the language and side they were made in.
- German voices were reviewed by the project, not by a native-speaker editor; corrections are welcome in the issues.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.
