# still online v0.2.0

The living internet: v0.1's desktop and Messenger grow into a small, fully local 2007, with a web to browse, mail to read, things to make and games to play, and days that actually pass.

## Highlights

- **Days pass.** Shut down and come back, or return on a new real date, and the world moves on: new chats, mail, guestbook entries and profile comments for days 1–5.
- **A fictional web.** Web Explorer, Searchly, Spaceport profiles, a cursed fan page, a gossip blog and a school site, all local and all invented.
- **Mail, TuneMole, Paint, Snapshot Booth and four games**, plus Fluff the sheep and Stapley.
- **A much richer Amplifier**: three skins, windowshade, a 10-band equalizer and an editable playlist.

## What's new by area

- **Desktop**: All Programs flyout with folders, Explorer with My Documents/Pictures/Music on a persistent virtual file system, a restorable Recycle Bin, balloon tips, a dial-up dialog with a synthesized handshake, Disk Cleanup, and the day shown on boot and in the clock tooltip.
- **Display Properties**: eleven procedural wallpapers (nine homages, two easter eggs), Blue/Olive/Silver appearance, and the Pipes, Flying Panes, Marquee and Slideshow screensavers.
- **Messenger**: day-aware scripts for all six friends, away messages and a "while you were away" summary, Message History, display pictures, six winks, more built-in emoticons and custom ones from your pictures, and reactions to both your music and your game scores.
- **Internet**: Web Explorer with history and favorites, Searchly, Spaceport (a profile per friend plus yours), the cursed fan page, a gossip blog, a school site, Mail with per-day content, and TuneMole.
- **Creative**: Paint, and Snapshot Booth with eight filters and a cartoon fallback. The webcam stays local: nothing is uploaded, and the camera stops when the window closes.
- **Amplifier**: skins, windowshade, equalizer with presets, playlist editing and a "Burn CD" joke.
- **Games and extras**: Mines, Hen Hunt, Star Pinball, Desktop Wrecker, Fluff and Stapley, each with persisted scores or settings where it makes sense.
- **Quality**: axe-checked accessibility for the new apps with keyboard paths, lazy-loaded apps with an initial JS budget of 250 KB gzip, and canvases that pause when hidden or minimized.

## Known issues

- Stories cover days 1–5; later days keep the day-5 state.
- Fluff can't be dropped onto a title bar at the very top of the screen.
- Mines dialogs are in-window overlays and feel cramped on Beginner.
- Pinball and Hen Hunt simulations are unit-tested, but scoring outcomes aren't asserted end to end in a browser.
- Not designed for phones or tablets; small screens only get a notice.
- Audio and sounds were verified by tests, not by ear, in the automated reviews.
