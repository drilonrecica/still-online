# still online: the week-two story bible (days 6–10)

Binding for W1–W3 (`tasks.md` → v0.5 rules). Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-v05-week-two-design.md`. If this file and the spec disagree, follow this file. Facts from days 1–5 never change (§9).

## 1. Ground rules

- **Calendar:** this is the second school week. Days have no weekday names, with one exception: the rec hall show is "saturday", as on day 1. The show is on **day 10**. Use "tmrw" or "tonight" for anything else.
- **Day 10 is show night.** Every day-10 chat, mail, post and photo is set **after the show**, the same way day 5 came after the concert. Only the PMs a friend starts day 10 with are pre-show (Marco's "so like 12").
- **Why the day-1 rec hall date slipped:** "bingo took the first date. bingo always wins" (Marco, day 6). The bean cellar was the warm-up.
- **Voices:**
  - Jess: ALL CAPS.
  - Kayla: ~\*drama\*~, with a new PM every day.
  - Theo: shy, says sorry.
  - Marco: dry, lowercase, calls the player "kid", never uses „!".
  - Pia: bossy, tells mom.
  - Dex: slow, few words, always **Away**.
- **Gender:**
  - Anything about or addressed to the player goes through `g()`.
  - A boy is a guy friend: teasing, (Y), "dude". He gets no hearts and no crush, and he is never inside a girls' plan.
  - A girl gets girly 2007 and Theo's small, PG crush step:
    - day 8: Jess's tease
    - day 9: "near the back"
    - day 10: they stand together
  - Both sides share ids, nodes, choice counts, days, links and story facts.
- **Script shape** (W1): one `onDay` script a day, 4–6 nodes, 2–3 choices per branch, a mid-script `personalMessageChange` and about 20–35 contact lines. In the tables, "PM a → b" means the day's starting PM, then the mid-script change.
- **Links:** `Xn` points to the cross-link index in §8. Each cross-link is defined there once, with its day.
