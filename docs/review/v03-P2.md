# v0.3 P2 — German content (cultural localization): gate review

## What was built

- **P2-01** `docs/localization-de.md`: the six German voices, 2007 German chat-speak, school mapping, storyline glossary, don'ts, parity rules.
- **P2-02 … P2-04** Full German overlays for all six friends (personal messages, away replies, day presence, every script node including game reactions and now-playing), structurally identical to English so old chats re-render after a switch.
- **P2-05** German seeded files (Bio-Referat, poems, Recycle Bin), photo captions, mail days 1–5 and per-friend auto-replies.
- **P2-06** German Spaceport profiles and all sites (Westfield-Gymnasium, Celeb Gossip 07 as a German gossip mag, Tinfoil Comets fan page, VirusGuard, Searchly with a German index, about.example).
- **P2-07** German tray balloons, Tacki's tips, TuneMole results, game names in chat placeholders; `src/content/fallback.test.ts` proves no English fallback is reachable in German.

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast` (a German browser picks Deutsch, or choose it in the login screen's **Language / Sprache** box).
2. Log on, open **Messenger** and sign in: Jess nudges in German. Answer her and the others.
3. Open **Mail** → **Senden/Empfangen**, then **Web Explorer** → `spaceport.example/jess`, `celebgossip07.example`, `westfield-high.example`.
4. Next day: **Start → Ausschalten**, click the black screen, log on again („Tag 2"). Repeat up to day 5.

## Fixes made in this gate

- **Content (~35 lines):** translation-shaped or unidiomatic lines rewritten in each voice (e.g. Dex „lol fair" → „lol verständlich", Theo „wer ist da?" → „wer bist du?", Jess „ZOOM" → „ZISCH", Kayla's „das is jetzt ein ding" → „so heißt das jetzt. offiziell."); Theo spells correctly and Dex says „fett" (not „nice") in other friends' profile comments; „klassiker" stays Marco's.
- **Register:** the ringtone spam now says „Sie"; the new-day balloon says „Ihre" and „persönliche Nachricht"; Theo's auto-replies lowercase like his chats.
- **Storyline terms:** Ziggy's trick is „drumstick-wirbel" everywhere (chat said „drehen").
- **Typography:** ~55 German quotes closed with ASCII `"` → `“` across `src/content/de`. Spaceport wrapped profile headlines in hard-coded English quotes; now catalog key `sites.spaceport.headlineQuoted` („…“ in German), with a test.
- **Layout:** Mail's message list pushed the Received column out of view once long subjects (chain letters) arrived, in both languages; subjects now end in an ellipsis (full subject as tooltip).
- Decisions logged in `docs/decisions.md` (P2-G lines).

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- On Kayla's profile Brianna asks about her „nachricht", while the invite in the story is an email; the English has the same gap („did u get my IM??"). Content follow-up for both languages.
- Mail lists Mama's day-1 mail first on later days (inbox ordering by `receivedAt`, identical in English); not a localization issue.
- A friend's day script only starts after the previous conversation is answered, so a player who ignores chats sees later days' scripts late. By design; worth a sentence in player-facing docs (P3-02).
