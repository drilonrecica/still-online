# M3 — Amplifier: gate review

## What was built

- **M3-01/02/03** Music candidates, the pick (5 CC BY tracks by Kevin MacLeod, 17 MB in `public/music/`), `CREDITS.md` attribution rows and `src/content/playlist.ts`.
- **M3-04** Playback store (`src/apps/amplifier/store.ts`): one `HTMLAudioElement`, play/pause/stop/next/prev, seek, persisted volume, shuffle, repeat, auto-advance, event-bus events, global mute.
- **M3-05** Amplifier main window: original dark-metal skin, LCD time (click toggles remaining), scrolling marquee, seek and volume sliders, transport buttons, keyboard control.
- **M3-06** Playlist panel (PL toggle grows the same window): numbered list, durations, current track highlighted, double-click / Enter plays, total time.
- **M3-07** Visualizer: 19 bars with falling peaks, click cycles bars → oscilloscope → off, static when paused or with reduced motion.
- **M3-08** Messenger integration: real `amplifier:trackChanged` events drive "now playing"; contacts react to it.
- **M3-09** Amplifier E2E (`tests/e2e/amplifier.spec.ts`).

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, boot, log on.
2. Double-click **Amplifier**, press ▶. Click the bars to switch to the oscilloscope, press **PL** for the playlist. Space plays/pauses, arrows seek and change volume.
3. Double-click **Messenger**, sign in, tick "Show what I'm listening to": your personal message becomes `♫ artist - title` and follows track changes.

## Fixes made in this gate

- **Amplifier title-bar buttons** (`_`, `×`) rendered ~75×23 px because xp.css gives every `button` a `min-width`/`min-height`; they now use `min-width/min-height: 0` (`Amplifier.module.css`).
- **Toast layout**: the body overflowed its box (content-box + padding) so message text ran under the `×`, and the close button inherited the xp.css minimum size. Now `border-box` with the minimums cleared (`Toast.module.css`).
- **Flaky amplifier e2e**: Jess's sign-in nudge deliberately opens and shakes her chat window, which could cover Messenger and intercept the checkbox click under load. The spec (and the gate spec) now waits for that chat and closes it first.
- Added `tests/e2e/review-m3.spec.ts`.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- Slider thumbs (seek/volume) use the light xp.css thumb on the dark skin; functional but not matched to the metal skin.
- In the compact (no playlist) state there is ~30 px of unused skin below the buttons; cosmetic.
- Real audio was not listened to in this headless gate (volume, mute and decode of the real MP3s are covered by unit tests and file presence only).
