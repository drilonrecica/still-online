# P6 — Games and cult: gate review

## What was built

- **P6-01** Games store (persisted best times and top-10 tables, sanitised `recordScore`) and **Mines**: seeded pure board logic (first click safe, flood reveal, chord, flag/?/none), LCD counters, smiley faces, Beginner/Intermediate/Expert/Custom, Best Times, `game:score` on a win.
- **P6-02** **Hen Hunt**: three-layer parallax countryside, hens at three depths, shells and reload, windmill/golden-hen/scarecrow bonuses, 90 s rounds, high scores. Simulation is pure TS and unit-tested.
- **P6-03** **Star Pinball**: fixed-timestep physics (no tunnelling), flippers, plunger, pop bumpers, drop targets with rank-ups, 3 balls, ball save, tilt, high scores.
- **P6-04** **Desktop Wrecker**: a transparent overlay with Hammer, Chainsaw, Flamethrower, Machine Gun, Stamp and Repair; nothing underneath changes; Esc exits.
- **P6-05** **Fluff the sheep**: a tray-toggled pet driven by a 20 Hz state machine that walks the taskbar and window title bars, falls, sleeps, bleats and can be thrown.
- **P6-06** **Stapley**: the stapler assistant with contextual tips, show-once, "don't show again", Hide, and the idle jokes.
- **P6-07** `onGameScore` trigger with `{score}`/`{game}` placeholders and one reaction per friend.

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `/?speed=fast`, boot and log on.
2. Stapley greets you on first boot. Open Start → All Programs → Games for **Mines**, **Hen Hunt**, **Star Pinball** and **Desktop Wrecker**.
3. Click the sheep icon in the tray to call **Fluff**; drag it onto a window's title bar.
4. Finish a game (e.g. win Mines) and open Messenger to see the friends' reactions.

## Fixes made in this gate

- **Mines cells were 23 px tall** (xp.css gives every `button` a 23 px minimum height), so the board overflowed the fixed-size window and the menubar and counters were hidden under the title bar. Cells and the smiley now set `min-height: 0`.
- **Mines dialogs were clipped**: the Best Times table overflowed the narrow Beginner window and the dialog's title bar was pushed out of view. The overlay now uses auto margins and scrolls, and the table stacks time over name.
- `tsconfig.json` and `tsconfig.node.json` gained the `@/*` path alias so e2e specs can import pure game logic from `src/` (used to replay a seeded Mines board).

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- Fluff can't be dropped onto a title bar that sits at the very top of the screen (its feet are clamped to its own height); in the review shot Notepad is moved down first. Windows spawn at y ≈ 25, so Fluff normally reaches them by walking and falling from a taller window only.
- Mines dialogs are in-window overlays rather than separate windows, so on Beginner they are cramped but readable.
- Pinball and Hen Hunt are exercised by input smoke tests plus unit-tested simulation; scoring outcomes aren't asserted end to end in a browser.
- Mid-round Hen Hunt shows score 0 in the review shot (no hen was hit by the scripted clicks); the HUD, shells and clock are all visible.
