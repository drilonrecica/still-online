## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, enter a name, pick **Girl** and **English**, log on.
2. **Day 1:** sign in to Messenger, open **Web Explorer** → **Connect**. Go to `pixlog.example/theo` and **Give kudos**. On `glittercodez.example`, **Copy code!!** a layout, then paste it under **Edit Profile → Layout code** on `friendspace.example/me` and **Save Changes**.
3. Start → **Turn Off Computer**, click to power on (day 2). In the Top 8 list of **Edit Profile**, remove Kayla. Turn off again (day 3): sign in to Messenger to see her react, add her back, then on `pixlog.example/me` use **Add photos** and **Edit album** for a caption.
4. Turn off (day 4). Start → All Programs → **User Accounts** → **Boy** → OK, and the tray's flag → **Deutsch**. Look at `/bulletins`, `/me`, `pixlog.example/me` (now the `night` skin), `pixlog.example/pia`, `glittercodez.example`, `cheatcave.example`, `kickflipkings.example` and Messenger.
5. Turn off („Ausschalten“, day 5): `/bulletins` → Jess's „NEUE FOTOS“ → kudos → the „COMETS LIVE!!!“ album, Jess's Friendspace page, Dex's Pixlog blog, `kickflipkings.example/video` and Jess's chat.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- **Saved Friendspace profile freezes default text.**
  - What happens: Save Changes in the editor stores the headline, mood and "About me" even when the player only pasted a layout. Those are then the girl's English defaults („giggly“, „new 2 Friendspace!! ~\*sparkle\*~ <3“, „Hi!! <3 I just joined Friendspace…“), and they stay after a switch to German or to a boy (`d4-03-me-de.png`).
  - This is the documented v0.3 behaviour (`docs/i18n.md`: `myProfile` is player data), but it goes against "persist only what the player typed".
  - Suggested fix: keep untouched fields absent (or compare them with the current default on save) and resolve them at render, like dirty files.
  - It changes the persisted shape, so it was left for v0.4.1.
- **One-off page-load stall:** once in four `@review` runs, Web Explorer stayed on "Opening page …" for over 10 s on day 1, right after GlitterCodez's Copy code. The load is a single scaled `setTimeout` (≤ 1.3 s) in `useNavigation.ts`. It didn't recur in three reruns or in the default suite. Worth watching in CI.
- **`/me` keeps the editor's scroll position** after Save Changes, so the page opens halfway down the wall (`d1-05`, `d2-03`). Scroll to the top on save.
- **„{name} has {n} friends in their Top 8.“** is neutral for every profile, friends included; friends have no gender in the data, so it stays.
- **Carried over from P3/P4:**
  - seeded photo date stamps (`'07 3 28` on the concert album)
  - long survey bulletins wrap under the sender picture
  - „adden“ is not on the allowed-anglicisms list
- **Unanswered chats carry over:** a script the player never answers waits for them, so Jess's day-1 opener is still first on day 5 when nobody replied. This is by design, but players who skip Messenger see old openers.
