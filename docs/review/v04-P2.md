# v0.4 P2 — Pixlog gate (self-review)

## What was built

- **P2-01 Pixlog shell:** `pixlog.example` with the orange look and shutter-"o" wordmark, the login splash, friend pages with `?tab=profile|photos|blog|surveys|guestbook`, the album page (large photos, prev/next, captions, day-gated comments), six skins, and Searchly indexing of the friend pages.
- **P2-02 Feed + visitors:** pure, seeded `shoutsFeed`, `visitors` and `visitorCount` in `logic.ts`; the home feed and the "Who visited my profile" box, localized through the catalog.
- **P2-03 `/me`:** kudos (once per friend per day, shown on the friend's page), your album built from My Pictures with editable captions, surveys you fill in and post, the skin picker (default follows gender), kudos received, the visitors box.
- **This gate:** a flow test `tests/e2e/v04-p2.spec.ts` (home feed, a friend's photos tab → album, kudos once a day, `/me` album + survey), the `@review` spec `tests/e2e/v04-review-p2.spec.ts`, and this document.

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, log on.
2. Open **Web Explorer**, click **Connect**.
3. `pixlog.example`: the feed and the visitors box. Content is day-gated, so on day 1 the feed is short; Jess's album appears on day 5.
4. `pixlog.example/jess?tab=photos`, then open the album. Press **Give kudos ★**; the button turns into "Kudos given today!".
5. `pixlog.example/me`: **Add photos** (picks from My Pictures), **Edit album** for captions, **Fill in a survey** → **Post to my profile**, and the skin swatches.
6. Switch the language with the tray badge and look again.
