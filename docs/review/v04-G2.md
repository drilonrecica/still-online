# v0.4 G2 — The boyish pass

G2 gives a boy his own 2007 without changing who the friends are. Jess still glitters, Kayla still has ~\*drama\*~ and Pia still loves ponies. What changes is what they say to him, what they pull him into and what he answers.

## What was built

- **G2-01:** the gender audit (`npm run gender-audit`, `src/content/genderAudit*.ts`). Jess and Kayla for a boy: the flames-and-drumstick concert sign, the band shirt and the hoodie with the hole, (Y) instead of (L), and the party part of Brianna's birthday instead of the sleepover („she said ur 'kinda funny'“).
- **G2-02:** Pia, Theo, Marco, Dex and the shared modules. He is Pia's annoyed big brother who still helps („a foal. obviously. can i go now“, the spit shake, the pony-jail guard). Marco's flyer gets a flaming skull, Theo offers him the Galaxy Raiders 3 cheat, and the survey asks about a buddy's.
- **G2-03:** `Script.forGender` and six boy-only threads for Marco (LAN party, cheat trade, roadie) and Dex (curb session, backyard wrestling, video night).
- **G2-04:** three procedural scenes as boy sides of shared photo slots (`skatepark_wipeout.jpg`, `lan_party_marcos.jpg`, `dexs_ramp.jpg`), boy captions on every photo, the neutral mouth on the `short` self-portrait, and a boy lyrics notebook („grounded“, „56k“).
- **G2-G:** this review, the boy read-through, `tests/e2e/v04-g2.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/v04-review-g2.spec.ts` and the `playChat`/`nextDay` helpers in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts`.

## How to try it

`npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast` (Messenger timers at 1/100), type a name, pick **Boy** and log on.

1. Open Messenger and sign in. Answer Jess until the concert sign, and Pia's horse quiz.
2. Start → Turn Off Computer → click → day 2. Answer Dex's day-2 chat; six fast minutes later he asks you to the gas-station curb.
3. Turn off again for day 3: Kayla and Brianna's party.
4. My Pictures, `skatepark_wipeout.jpg`, My Documents → `song_lyrics_notebook.txt`, and `pixlog.example/me` → Add photos.
5. Switch to Girl in User Accounts (or the tray language to German) and look again.

- **Nothing girly is aimed at him.** What a boy reads from the cue list is a friend's own glitter, outfit, lip gloss, ponies and personal messages: Jess's „disco ball mode (L)“, Pia's „pretty“ about her own ponies, Kayla's „<3“ PMs. The rest is story facts (Brianna's pink envelopes, the „cute pm“), and he answers those in his own voice: to Kayla's „ugh BOYS“ he says „its 2 words kayla. its fine“.

The boy threads fit them: the LAN party is on friday because the show is on saturday, and the roadie only does the load-in.

- **Content rubric:** the voices hold on both sides. Jess is ALL CAPS and nudges, Kayla's ~\*PMs\*~ keep changing, Marco is dry, lowercase and never „!“, Theo apologises, Pia is bossy and tells mom, Dex is slow and always Away. There are only `.example` domains and invented games, bands and brands, and no new anachronism.
- **Gender rubric:**
  - Played the same days as a girl and as a boy. The girl's side is unchanged or girlier („ZIGGY MARRY ME“, BIG pink, the counter sleepover with face masks). The boy's side has its own jokes, not swapped nouns: flames and drumsticks, the movie night with 2 kinds of pizza, the foal, the curb, the Modem Mauler.
