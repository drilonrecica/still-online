# v0.5 W3 — German, days 6–10

W3 replaced every week-two placeholder with real German: the chats, gender-only threads and presence (W3-01), and the mail, sites, Friendspace and Pixlog (W3-02). This gate played days 6–10 in German as a girl and as a boy, had the text reviewed against the German style guide and fixed what came up.

## What was built

- **W3-01 (chats):** `src/content/de/contacts/<friend>-week2.ts` for all six friends, with day presence, the scripts for days 6–10 and that friend's week-two gender-only threads. The glossary in `docs/localization-de.md` gained a "v0.5: week two" section.
- **W3-02 (mail, sites, social):**
  - German mail for days 6–10
  - the week-two text of Westfield High, Tinfoil Comets, Kickflip Kings, Celeb Gossip and CheatCave
  - Friendspace bulletins, walls and band pages
  - the Pixlog albums, blogs, shouts and guestbooks
  - No `W3 replaces` comment is left in `src/content/de`.
- **W3-G (this gate):**
  - `tests/e2e/v05-w3.spec.ts` (default suite, `de-DE` locale) checks day 10 in German for a girl and for a boy:
    - Marco's day-10 chat is in German, with no English left
    - Westfield's co-reps
    - Marco's show album caption (front row or crew)
    - the band page's „gespielt. 83 leute“
    - Mom's mail: „Hallo Schatz,“ / „Hallo mein Großer,“ and the Dex line for each side
  - `tests/e2e/v05-review-w3.spec.ts` (`@review`) takes the screenshots below.

## How to try it

`npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, pick **Deutsch** (the German flag) on the login screen, type a name, pick **Mädchen** or **Junge** and log on. Turn Off Computer (**Ausschalten**) once per day to move forward. Alternatively, from devtools, set `still-online:v1:world` → `state.day` and reload.

On days 6–10:

- Sign in to Messenger: every friend has a chat each day.
- Open Mail and press **Senden/Empfangen**.
- In Web Explorer, visit the sites from the W2 review: `westfield-high.example/news`, `tinfoilcomets.example`, `kickflipkings.example/video`, `celebgossip07.example`, `cheatcave.example/clips`, `friendspace.example/bulletins`, `friendspace.example/band/velvet-lantern` and `pixlog.example/<friend>`.

Switching the language from the tray (`EN`/`DE`) re-renders chats and mail that have already arrived.

### German content rubric

Four reviewers read every German week-two file next to its English twin, the style guide (`docs/localization-de.md`) and the story bible: Jess, Kayla and Pia; Theo, Marco and Dex; mail and sites; Friendspace and Pixlog.

- **Voices** sound German, not translated:
  - Jess's caps and nudges („ICH HAB GEGEN NUDELN VERLOREN“)
  - Kayla's ~\*Drama\*~ and her daily status message
  - Dex is short and Abwesend all week
  - Theo's „sorry“ and „ähm“
  - Marco is lowercase and dry. A grep finds no „!“ in any of his lines; the only one is the player's „83 leute!!!“.
  - Pia is bossy („mama alles erzählen is MEIN job“)
- **2007:** Kassetten, Camcorder, the library printer, TicketTown's busy line, LAN parties. There are no anachronisms.
- **Address forms:** grown-ups write like grown-ups. Mama writes „Hab dich lieb“, Papa says „Aha. Schön.“, and the school writes to the „Schülerinnen und Schüler“. Friends and sites say „du“, and the system UI says „Sie“.
- **Typography:** umlauts and ß are right, and quotes are „…“. Jess's and Pia's week-two chats closed eight quotes with a straight `"`; these now use `“`.
- **Story facts match the English:** 83 people in a room for 80, 61 zu 61, Jess 2nd behind the Makkaroni-Keytar, Dex 3rd, Sir Sternenstaub der Zweite, Pia not at the show, and Lakeview sold out the same night.
- **Terms** are the same across chat, mail, sites and social. The fixes below closed the gaps the reviewers found.

- **Flaky screenshot run:** in one run the review spec's toast-closing loop failed once for one shot (Dex, girl). The rerun passed 16/16. The loop races sign-in toasts that close on their own; it is only in the `@review` spec.
- **Left as they were:**
