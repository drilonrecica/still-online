# P2 — Living world: gate review

## What was built

- **P2-01** Day-aware Messenger: `onDay` triggers, per-day presence (`Contact.days`, persisted `presenceDay`), `ContactLine.unlock`.
- **P2-02** Days 2–5 for Jess (concert week) and Kayla (the Brianna saga).
- **P2-03** Days 2–5 for Dex (the skate video) and Theo (the cell project).
- **P2-04** Days 2–5 for Marco (the Velvet Lantern gig) and Pia (the pony crisis; day 5 unlocks `ponies`).
- **P2-05** New-day moments: "Day N" on the boot screen, `<date> · Day N` in the clock tooltip, the "A new day!" balloon, and the first-boot welcome balloon.

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `/?speed=fast` (the fast clock scales every delay by 1/100), boot, log on and open Messenger → Sign in.
2. **Start → Turn Off Computer**, click the off screen to power on: the boot screen reads "Day 2". Log on, sign in to Messenger again; friends have new personal messages and new chats start.
3. Repeat to reach days 3–5. Pia's day-5 gift unlocks the pony wallpapers (Display Properties → Desktop).

## Fixes made in this gate

- **Messenger stayed signed in across a power cycle** (`src/apps/messenger/runtime.ts`). The engine's `signedIn` is in-memory, so Turn Off → power on in the same tab kept it signed in: contacts never re-signed in, so days 2+ got no presence changes and no `onDay` scripts. Leaving the desktop (log off or shut down) now signs the engine out; the next sign-in applies the new day. Unit test added in `runtime.test.ts`.
- Added `tests/e2e/v02-p2.spec.ts` and `tests/e2e/review-p2.spec.ts`.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- After a power cycle the player must click **Sign in** in Messenger again (same as a fresh page load); Messenger does not auto-sign-in.
- Day-5 content is the last authored day; day 6+ reuses the day-5 presence and shows the generic "Your friends are up to something." balloon.
- The `onDay` scripts of one contact queue behind each other on a catch-up (e.g. skipping a day); they play in order, but long catch-ups were not played through in this gate.
