# v0.4 G — Player gender gate (self-review)

## What was built

- **G-01** The login screen asks „I am a: Girl / Boy“ („Ich bin: Mädchen / Junge“) under the name. Log On waits for both, and old saves are asked once. There are eight original user tiles, and the default follows the gender. A new **User Accounts** app (Start → All Programs → System Tools) changes the name, gender and picture live.
- **G-02** The gendered content engine: `g(girl, boy)` and `gendered({ girl, boy })` write both versions into the content, and `contentFor(locale, gender)` resolves them. Messenger, files, mail and sites re-render on a gender switch like a language switch. Lint, parity and the playthrough run girl/boy × en/de (`docs/gender.md`).
- **G-03** Every personal seed slot has a girl and a boy file (My Documents, Recycle Bin). The webcam self-portrait has long or short hair. The Friendspace player profile, the Snapshot Booth look and the Messenger picture colours are per gender.
- **G-04** Chats and mail per gender. Lines about the player change, girl-talk becomes boy-talk for a boy, and a boy has no crush. Mom's mails say „Hi buddy“ / „Hallo mein Großer“ and ask about helmets. Boys get the CheatCave Weekly newsletter and cheat-CD spam. German uses natural gendered forms.
- **G-05** CheatCave (`cheatcave.example`): news, cheats, the top 10 clips and a forum (Theo, Dex and Marco), day-gated, EN + DE.
- **G-06** Kickflip Kings (`kickflipkings.example`): news and shoutbox, crew, tricks, Dex's day-5 video and Velvet Lantern gigs. Per-gender default favorites (untouched ones follow the gender) and Searchly's „hot sites this week“ box.
- **This gate:** `tests/e2e/v04-g.spec.ts` (default suite) and the `@review` spec `tests/e2e/v04-review-g.spec.ts`.

## How to try it

1. Run `npm run dev` and open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`. Enter a name, pick **Boy** and log on.
2. **My Documents** has `my_wrestling_entrance_speech.txt` and `top 8 (FINAL final).txt`. The **Recycle Bin** has `cheat_codes.txt` and `my_rap_lyrics.txt`. In **My Pictures**, open `me_webcam_test.jpg` to see the short hair.
3. **Web Explorer → Favorites** lists CheatCave and Kickflip Kings. Searchly's home page puts them first under „Hot sites this week!“.
4. In **Messenger**, answer Theo. A couple of minutes later he asks plant or animal cell. Pick „plant cell!“: the boy answers „lol no beans r legit. bean cell“ where a girl says „lol no thats cute“.
5. **Start → All Programs → System Tools → User Accounts**, pick **Girl**, then OK. Open windows re-render in place: untouched files, favorites, chats and mail. Files you edited keep their text.
6. **Turn Off Computer** four times to reach day 5. Mom's „who is Dex?“ mail arrives, and `kickflipkings.example/video` shows the premiere.

- **Gender rubric:**
  - I played day 1 (Theo) and day 5 (mail, sites) as a girl and as a boy.
  - A content pass over every `g()` in files, chats, mail, profiles and sites, in EN and DE, found no crush on the boy side and no „Mädchen“ words for him. The boy side has its own topics: the wrestling speech, cheat codes, the console chain mail, movie night, CheatCave Weekly, helmets.

- **Content rubric:** voices hold in every `g()` line. Marco has no „!“, and Theo apologises. There are no anachronisms, only `.example` domains, and only invented games and brands after the fixes below.
- **Leftover-literal check** (§P1 step 5) over `src/os` and `src/apps`: the same 9 hits as at the v0.3 gates, all proper nouns or false positives (xp.css `aria-label` hooks in `Window.tsx`, the wordmark, the Searchly logo, the `http://` placeholder, a code comment, expression matches).
