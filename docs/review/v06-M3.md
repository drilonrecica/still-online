# v0.6 M3 — games on touch gate

M3 makes the four games playable by touch and plays the story through on a phone. This gate checked every game on a phone (412×915 portrait, 915×412 landscape) and a tablet (1024×768), in English and German, with one phone set as a boy. It re-ran the M3-02 playthrough on both phone orientations. It applied the gate procedure and the touch rubric.

## What was built

- **M3-01 (games on touch):**
  - **`ScaleToFit`** (`src/os/touch/ScaleToFit.tsx`): a fixed-size stage zoomed with CSS `zoom` to fit its box, centred. `toStage` maps pointer coordinates (documented in `docs/touch.md`).
  - **Star Pinball:** the 460×640 window zoomed on a phone. Each half of the table works its flipper (multi-touch), and a hold on the launch lane pulls the plunger.
  - **Hen Hunt:** the 800×540 field zoomed on a phone. A tap aims and shoots (the crosshair shows for 300 ms), a one-finger drag pans, and a tap with an empty gun reloads.
  - **Mines:** pointer events, the 🚩 flag-mode toggle („Markiermodus"), long-press to flag and tap-to-chord. The board grows on a phone so Beginner cells are finger-sized, and shrinks to fit on a tablet.
  - **Desktop Wrecker:** Reset (R) and Use (Space) buttons once a finger touched the overlay.
- **M3-02:** the Konami code as swipes on the desktop background, Fluff dragged and petted by touch, and `tests/e2e/v06-playthrough.spec.ts`: day 1 as a girl in English and day 10 as a boy in German, by touch only.
- **M3-G (this gate):**
  - `tests/e2e/v06-m3.spec.ts` (default suite):
    - **Phone, portrait and landscape:**
      - A rotation mid-game keeps Mines' board (an opened cell stays open, a long-press still flags) and a Hen Hunt round going, with the stage on screen.
      - Expert fits the screen.
      - Every game's own buttons are at least 36×36 px: Mines' menu, face and toggle, Hen Hunt's and Pinball's menu and Back buttons, and every Wrecker palette button.
    - **Tablet:** Mines in a floating window: a tap reveals, a long-press flags, Expert fits at its own size. Pinball has no zoomed stage and launches by touch.
    - **Desktop (1280×800):** Mines, Hen Hunt and Pinball have no stage wrapper and no flag-mode button. Mines' cells stay 16 px, and a right-click flags, as in v0.5.
  - `tests/e2e/v06-review-m3.spec.ts` (`@review`) takes the screenshots below:
    - It plays each game by touch in either language.
    - It measures the games' buttons on a phone.
    - It lists every visible control under 36 px per shot as a `small-targets.json` attachment.
    - It fails on any console or page error, sideways page scroll, or a stage that leaves the screen.

    All runs pass on every device.

## How to try it

`npm run dev`, then open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast` in Chrome DevTools device mode (Pixel 7, then rotate; or a 1024×768 touch tablet). On a real phone, run `npm run dev -- --host` and open the network URL on the same Wi-Fi.

- **Games:** tap a desktop icon, or Start → All Programs → Games.
- **Mines:** tap to open, long-press (or turn on 🚩 and tap) to flag, and tap a number to chord. Game → Expert zooms the big board to fit.
- **Hen Hunt:** Play, then tap a hen to shoot. Drag the field to look around, and tap with an empty gun to reload.
- **Star Pinball:** Play, then hold the launch lane (bottom right) and let go. Tap either half for its flipper, and tap the score display to nudge.
- **Desktop Wrecker:** tap a tool and then the screen. Reset (R), Use (Space) and Exit are on the palette.

Run the review with `npx playwright test v06-review-m3 --grep @review --project phone` (also `phone-landscape` and `tablet`), and the playthrough with `npx playwright test v06-playthrough --project phone` (also `phone-landscape`).

## Fixes made in this gate

All fixes are scoped to phones (`[data-device='phone']`) or decided per touch event, so the tablet and the desktop are unchanged.

- **Buttons inside a zoomed stage:** they shrank with the zoom. Hen Hunt's Play / High Scores / Help were 20 px tall in portrait (zoom ≈ 0.5), and Mines' flag-mode toggle was 35 px in landscape.
  - `ScaleToFit` now sets `--stage-scale` on the stage, and the games size these controls as `calc(36px / var(--stage-scale, 1))`.
  - This covers Hen Hunt's and Pinball's menu and panel buttons, and Mines' face and toggle (never under the classic 26 px).
- **Mines' Game menu:** the button was 23 px tall. The menu and its items are now 36 px, as in Notepad.
- **Desktop Wrecker's palette:** the tool icons were 32×33 px and the Reset / Use / Exit buttons 23 px tall. The overlay is portalled outside `.desktop-root`, so it now carries `data-device` itself. On a phone every palette button is 36 px, and the tools sit in 36 px columns.
- **Star Pinball's nudge was keyboard-only (T).**
  - A finger on the dot-matrix score display now nudges the table. A mouse there still works the flippers, as in v0.5.
  - Help gets a „Without a mouse" line on touch devices (EN + DE), worded like Hen Hunt's.
  - Unit-tested in `Pinball.test.tsx`.
- Logged in `docs/decisions.md` (M3-G) and `docs/touch.md`.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- **Mines' cells under 36 px where the field can't fit them:**
  - Expert on a phone: 13–14 px.
  - Beginner on a landscape phone: 22 px. Nine 36 px rows need 324 px, and about 300 px are left under the title bar, menu bar and taskbar.
  - Flag mode, long-press flagging and chording keep the small boards playable. Portrait Beginner cells are over 36 px, as M3-01 required.
- **Portrait letterbox:** Hen Hunt (zoom ≈ 0.5) and Pinball keep their aspect ratio, so a portrait phone shows the window face above and below. Hen Hunt's help text is small at that zoom, and its Back button is tall with small text. Landscape is the better way to play Hen Hunt.
- **Tablet game buttons** (Mines' face and toggle, about 23 px) keep their desktop size. The 36 px rule is for phones, and the tablet rubric covers title bars, grips and the taskbar.
- **Pinball's help panel** overlaps the bottom edge of the score display in a landscape phone in German (the longest text). It is an overlay, so nothing is hidden that matters.
- The M2 gaps (Notepad's menu widths, Mail's column header, Regional's tab, tablet dialog buttons, the tablet Amplifier title bar, the tray badge) are unchanged.
