  - this document

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, pick **Deutsch** at login, then a name and Mädchen or Junge.
2. Open **Web Explorer** and click **Verbinden**.
3. Each day, look at `friendspace.example/bulletins`, the `pixlog.example` feed and the friend of the day: Theo (1), Dex (2), Kayla (3), Pia (4), Jess (5). Look at both their Pixlog page and their Friendspace profile. On day 5 also see every blog and survey (`pixlog.example/<friend>?tab=blog|surveys`), the albums, the four bands and `glittercodez.example`.
4. To jump to a day, set `still-online:v1:world` → `state.day` in localStorage (the review spec does this), or play day by day with „Ausschalten“.
