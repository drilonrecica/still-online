# v0.5 W4 — release-ready gate

W4 closes v0.5 (week two, days 6–10). W4-01 wrote the release e2e flows, the README, a v0.5 section in `docs/architecture-v0.4.md` and `docs/release-notes-v0.5.0.md`. This gate played the week the way a player would on one save:

1. Days 6–8 in English as a girl.
2. Switch to Boy in User Accounts and to Deutsch in the tray.
3. Days 9–10 in German as a boy.

Each day covers Messenger, Mail, every week-two site, Friendspace and Pixlog. Nothing was tagged, pushed or released.

## What was built

- **W4-01:** `tests/e2e/v05-w4.spec.ts` (day 10 as a girl, as a boy, and in German), the README (days 1–10, week two, known gaps), the architecture section and the release notes (English and „Auf Deutsch“).
- **W4-G (this gate):**
  - `tests/e2e/v05-w4.spec.ts` gained the gate's main flow, in the default suite: a girl on day 8 in English reads Jess's „Ada better come too“ bulletin. She switches to Boy and Deutsch, shuts down and boots into „Tag 9“. Then:
    - the bulletin re-renders as „Ada kommt gefälligst mit“
    - `/me` shows the boy's German default headline („skaten bis zum umfallen“)
    - Kayla's „~\*61 zu 61\*~ (HILFE)“ arrives in Mail
  - `tests/e2e/v05-review-w4.spec.ts` (`@review`) plays the whole week on one save and takes the screenshots below. It asserts there are no console errors, warnings or page errors.

## How to try it

`npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, type a name, pick **Girl** and log on. To jump to day 6, set `still-online:v1:world` → `state.day` to `6` in devtools and reload. Otherwise, play days 1–5 first and Turn Off Computer once per day.

- **Days 6–8:** sign in to Messenger and answer every friend. Open Mail and press **Send/Recv**. In Web Explorer, visit:
  - `westfield-high.example/news`
  - `tinfoilcomets.example`
  - `kickflipkings.example` (`/video` from day 9)
  - `celebgossip07.example`
  - `cheatcave.example` (`/clips` on day 10)
  - `friendspace.example/bulletins` and a friend's page (`friendspace.example/<friend>`)
  - `pixlog.example/<friend>`
- **The switch (end of day 8):** Start → All Programs → System Tools → **User Accounts** → **Boy** → OK. Then click `EN` in the tray and pick **Deutsch**. Open My Documents: the boy's German files are there.
- **Days 9–10:** **Ausschalten**, log on, and do the same in German. On day 10, also open:
  - `friendspace.example/band/velvet-lantern`
  - `pixlog.example/album?id=marco-rec-hall&n=6`
  - `friendspace.example/me`
