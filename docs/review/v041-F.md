# v0.4.1 F — Fixes

Phase F fixes the known gaps the v0.4 reviews listed. It adds no new features. Release notes: [`docs/release-notes-v0.4.1.md`](../release-notes-v0.4.1.md).

## What was built

- **F-01 (player profile):** Friendspace `/me` saves only the fields the player typed. The headline, mood, "About me" and theme fill from the current language's and gender's defaults at render. v0.4 saves holding default text are cleaned up in `sanitizeProfile` against a frozen table (`src/apps/browser/legacyProfile.ts`). `/me` scrolls to the top after Save. The player's picture on Friendspace `/me`, in friends' Top 8 and on Pixlog `/me` is the User Accounts tile, and it updates live.
- **F-02 (content polish, EN + DE):**
  - The boy's last four diary entries are his own.
  - Boy favorites: Searchly, CheatCave, Kickflip Kings, Friendspace, Westfield High. The v0.4 order is kept as a legacy untouched list.
  - The boy's mood is „bored" (N) / „gelangweilt", and the girl's German mood is „albern".
  - Jess counts the concert down in days.
  - American spellings (`homework_math.txt`, `beach_vacation.jpg`), with v0.2 fingerprints so untouched saves stay untouched.
  - The German song rhyme, „adden" in the allowed anglicisms, and the day-2 Pixlog mail without a count.
- **F-03 (visual fixes):**
  - `PhotoSpec.day` and story-dated stamps (the concert reads `'07 10 11`).
  - `word-spacing: 2px` on reply buttons.
  - Bulletin bodies in one column.
  - The transcript stays at its last line after a language flip.
  - Stapley's balloon folds away after 8 s while the active window covers his corner.
- **F-04 (stale follow-ups):** an `afterScript` follow-up whose target started on an earlier day than a since-started `onDay` script of the contact no longer runs. Same-day chains and the boy-only threads are unchanged (`docs/content-format.md`).
- **F-G:** this review, `tests/e2e/v041-f.spec.ts` (default suite) and `tests/e2e/v041-review-f.spec.ts` (`@review`), plus the release notes.

## How to try it

`npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast` (Messenger timers at 1/100), type a name, pick **Girl** and log on.

1. Web Explorer → `friendspace.example/me` → Edit Profile, paste `[layout bg=starry]` into "Layout code:", Save Changes. The page jumps to the top.
2. Start → All Programs → User Accounts: pick **Boy** (or another picture), OK. `/me` shows his headline, mood and picture; `pixlog.example/me` shows the same picture. Switch the tray language to Deutsch, and the defaults turn German.
3. `friendspace.example/bulletins`: Kayla's „20 qs about me" survey stays in one column.
4. Messenger: answer Kayla until her status poll offers „1 obviously"; switch the language mid-chat and the transcript stays at its last line.
5. Maximize Web Explorer while Stapley talks: his balloon folds away after a few seconds. Click him to read it again.
6. Turn Off Computer four times to reach day 5 → `pixlog.example/album?id=jess-comets-show`: the stamp reads `'07 10 11`.
7. On day 1, leave Dex's follow-ups unanswered, go to day 2 and finish his chat. „tried the kickflip again. landed it 0 times" never arrives.
