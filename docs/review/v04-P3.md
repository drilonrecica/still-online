# v0.4 P3 — English content gate (self-review)

## What was built

- **P3-01 Friendspace content:** 15 bulletins over days 1–5. They include chain letters (luck, a gendered crush/high-score letter, "if u dont repost", Marco's "not a chain letter"), Kayla's and Jess's 20-question surveys, the Tinfoil Comets countdown and aftermath, Pia's pony club, and "NEW PICS on pixlog!!" on Dex's, Pia's, Jess's and Kayla's album days. There are four bands: Tinfoil Comets, Velvet Lantern, The Grip Tapes and The Sugarcube Parade. Each friend has Top 8 drop/re-add Messenger scripts and wall lines, and a few wall comments now carry comment graphics.
- **P3-02 / P3-03 Pixlog content:** every friend has a skin, an about text, 1–2 albums, 3–4 blog posts, a survey (three shared surveys in `pixlog/surveys.ts`), shouts on each day, kudos days and guestbooks. Everything is gated to the day the chat reveals it.
- **P3-04 GlitterCodez + mail:** 12 layouts (6 girly, 6 boyish) with 2007 blurbs, the glitter/sticker/cursor page text, and the mails `d2-pixlog-visitors` and `d4-friendspace-bulletin`.
- **This gate:** the flow spec `tests/e2e/v04-p3.spec.ts` (day 5: a "NEW PICS" bulletin leads to Jess's Pixlog and concert album, plus a band page and the 12 layouts; day 1: none of the day-5 news is out). Also the `@review` spec `tests/e2e/v04-review-p3.spec.ts`, a full rubric read of all six friends, and this document.

## How to try it

1. `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast`, log on (girl or boy).
2. Open **Web Explorer** and click **Connect**.
3. Each day, look at `friendspace.example/bulletins`, the `pixlog.example` feed and the friend whose news fits the day: Theo (1), Dex (2), Kayla (3), Pia (4), Jess (5). On day 5 also see each friend's albums and blog, `friendspace.example/band/<tinfoil-comets|velvet-lantern|grip-tapes|sugarcube-parade>` and `glittercodez.example`.
4. To jump to a day without playing through, set `still-online:v1:world` → `state.day` in localStorage (the review spec does this), or play day by day with Shut Down.

## Known gaps and follow-ups

- **German:** all P3 modules are placeholders (English text) until P4. That includes the German Top 8 overlay for Pia, which now carries the `g()` fix.
- **Marco's day-1 chat (pre-P3):** it still announces "velvet lantern @ the rec hall, saturday" (`src/content/contacts/marco.ts:101`, `:118`, and `files/personalDocs.ts:109`). Since v0.3 everything else says the bean cellar, one night only. A one-line venue change in Marco's day-1 chat (EN + DE) would settle it.
- **Calendar (pre-P3):** Jess says the concert is "next friday" / "in like a week" on day 1 and "3 more days" in her status. The concert falls between days 4 and 5, and the day-4 mail puts bio presentations on day 5 "first period". A separate timeline pass is needed.
- **Photo scenes:** there are only 8 procedural scenes, so a few captions sit on a loose fit, e.g. Theo's jello cell and Dex's living-room premiere both use `party`. The camera date stamps are seeded, not story dates (e.g. `'07 3 28` on the concert). A `classroom`/`livingroom` scene and story-dated stamps would help.
- **GlitterCodez previews:** some glitter titles are low-contrast on their own backgrounds (Pony Pastel, Leopard Luxe, Hearts 4 Ever). That's in character for a 2007 glitter site, and the tile name below each preview is always readable.
- **Mail `d2-pixlog-visitors`:** it says "3 new visitors" as the spec asks, but the visitors box on day 2 lists more names.
- **Theo and Marco's day-5 albums** get no "NEW PICS" bulletin (by design: only Dex, Pia, Jess, Kayla).
- Stapley's balloon still covers the bottom-right of the browser in screenshots (existing behaviour).
