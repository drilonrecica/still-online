## How to try it

`npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:5173/still-online/?speed=fast` (Messenger timers at 1/100), type a name, pick **Girl** or **Boy** and log on. Turn Off Computer once per day to move forward, and sign in to Messenger each day. On days 6–10 every friend has a chat. The gender-only threads follow a few (fast) seconds after their day's chat:

- girl: Jess on days 8 and 9, Kayla on day 9
- boy: Marco on days 7 and 10, Dex on day 8

Theo's day-10 chat adds the poster wallpaper to Display Properties.
